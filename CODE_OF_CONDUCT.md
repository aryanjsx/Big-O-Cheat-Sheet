# Code of Conduct

We want BigOSheet to be a welcoming, useful project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack or harass people.
- Share accurate, relevant information and cite sources for complexity claims.
- Accept feedback and focus discussion on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming to the maintainer, Aryan Kumar, through the contact options on [GitHub](https://github.com/aryanjsx). Include links or context where possible. Reports will be reviewed privately and handled as fairly as possible.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
